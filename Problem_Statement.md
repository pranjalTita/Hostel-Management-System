​The Hostel Management System is designed to make hostel management easier for students and institute hostel staff. An institute hostel currently manages student accommodation, room allocation, hostel fees, and complaints manually. Students have to contact the hostel office offline for room availability, fee-related issues, complaints, or to report a problem. Additionally, the hostel warden also faces problems maintaining offline records of all students. The offline system is difficult for students as well as hostel administration staff.
​The proposed HMS will provide a centralized system for managing hostel-related activities. Students will be able to check the availability of rooms, view their room information, check fee details, make payments, submit complaints, etc. The warden will be able to manage student records, room availability, payments, and complaints. The system will manage records of all students, update room information, and register complaints. This system reduces manual work and makes hostel management easier and faster.
​The system will also provide notifications related to events, group gatherings, or any function organized by the hostel. Hostel staff can also manage and view their profiles from the dashboard and receive notifications. Overall, the system will make hostel management easier for students and hostel staff by keeping all information in one place.
