# Problem Statement

## Hostel Management System (HMS)

The proposed Hostel Management System (HMS) is a digital solution designed to replace the institute's existing manual accommodation process with a centralized and efficient platform. Currently, the dependence on paper-based registers creates considerable inefficiency for both students and administrative staff. Students are required to visit the hostel office in person for routine tasks such as checking room availability, clearing fee dues, or registering maintenance complaints, while wardens must manage extensive administrative work through manual record-keeping.

The proposed system addresses these challenges by providing an integrated platform through which students can check room status, make fee payments online, track the resolution of complaints, and receive notifications about hostel events. At the same time, the administrative dashboard gives wardens and institute staff centralized access to student records, room allocation, fee management, and profile updates.

By reducing manual dependencies, minimizing errors, and improving resource utilization, the HMS aims to enhance transparency and provide a more efficient and user-friendly hostel management experience for the entire institute.

## Key Features

**For Students**
- Real-time room availability status
- Online fee payment and transaction history
- Complaint/grievance logging and resolution tracking
- Notifications for hostel events and announcements

**For Wardens / Administration**
- Centralized student database and profile management
- Real-time room allocation and occupancy tracking
- Fee collection and financial reconciliation
- Complaint management dashboard

## Objective

To digitize and centralize hostel operations, reducing administrative overhead, minimizing manual errors, and improving transparency and convenience for both students and staff.
